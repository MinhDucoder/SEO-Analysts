Semantic Keyword Clustering:
A Guide to Modern SEO

Master the shift from individual keyword targeting to comprehensive topic authority using advanced data grouping.

Alex Riverton

Director of Growth • 8 min read • Updated Oct 24, 2023

The Death of Single-Keyword Targeting

In the early days of SEO, ranking was a matter of repetition. You found a high-volume keyword, sprinkled it across your page, and waited for the crawler to reward your diligence. Today, search engines have evolved. Algorithms like BERT and MUM aren't looking for matches; they are looking for intent.

Keyword clustering is the process of grouping keywords into "themes" based on user intent. This allows you to build topical authority that is much harder for competitors to replicate.

“
The future of search isn't finding more information—it's finding information you can actually act upon... semantic clustering is the roadmap to that clarity.
— Alex Riverton

Three Pillars of Semantic Grouping

  • check Intent Alignment: Differentiating between "how-to" informational searches and "best of" transactional searches.
  • check LSI Mapping: Incorporating Latent Semantic Indexing terms that naturally occur within high-quality content.
  • check Internal Link Architecture: Creating a "Pillar and Spoke" model that shares link equity across a cluster.

How to Audit Your Existing Content

Most websites are sitting on a goldmine of unoptimized semantic data. By auditing your current rankings, you can identify pages that are "almost" ranking for high-intent clusters and push them over the finish line.
